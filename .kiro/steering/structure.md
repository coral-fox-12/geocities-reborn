## Rules
- The `/.kiro` directory stays at the repo root and is committed. Never add
  it to `.gitignore` (Devpost rules: its absence can disqualify the entry).
- New files go in the folder above that matches their kind. No new top-level
  folders without updating this file.
- Sample pages in `public/samples/` are generated by `generator.js`, never
  hand-written, so the gallery always shows what the generator really does.
